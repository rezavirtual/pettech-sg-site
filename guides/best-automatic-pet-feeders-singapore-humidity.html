Frequently Asked Singapore Pet Questions

How often should I change the desiccant bag in Singapore?
In Singapore's 80–90% ambient humidity, desiccant silica gel packets should be replaced every 14 to 21 days for maximum crunchiness.
Do these feeders work with local 230V Singapore power outlets?
Yes. All featured models run on standard 5V USB power supplies with emergency dual battery backup in case of local power trips.
