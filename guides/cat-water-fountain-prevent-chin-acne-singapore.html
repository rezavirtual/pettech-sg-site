Replacing porous plastic with surgical SUS304 stainless steel is the single most effective environmental change you can make to clear and prevent bacterial chin acne.

Frequently Asked Singapore Pet Questions

Why is stainless steel better than ceramic for cat acne?
Ceramic bowls can crack micro-glazes that harbor bacterial colonies. Medical-grade 304 stainless steel is non-porous, dishwasher sterilizable, and completely inhibits bacterial adhesion.
How often should filters be changed in Singapore tap water?
Singapore PUB tap water is moderately soft, but warm temperatures encourage bio-slime. Quadruple-filtration cartridges should be rinsed weekly and replaced every 3 to 4 weeks.
