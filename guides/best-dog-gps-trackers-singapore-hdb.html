Frequently Asked Singapore Pet Questions

Do satellite GPS collars work inside HDB lifts and stairwells?
Inside enclosed concrete lift shafts, GPS drops to cellular tower triangulation. Once outside the void deck or lift lobby, high-accuracy satellite lock resumes within 8–15 seconds.
Are monthly subscriptions required for Singapore GPS collars?
Yes, cellular GPS collars use a built-in eSIM to connect to Singtel/StarHub/M1. Monthly subscriptions range from S$6.00 to S$12.00 SGD/month for unlimited real-time tracking.
